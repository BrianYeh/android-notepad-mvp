# Just Notes v1.0.8 English-Market Expansion Design

**Date:** 2026-07-23

**Status:** Approved direction; Play Console execution requires final price and publication confirmation

**Target:** Expand the existing production release from Taiwan to an initial group of English-language markets without uploading a new AAB.

## 1. Goal

Increase the discoverable market for Just Notes while keeping the rollout small enough to validate store conversion, stability, and subscription behavior before wider distribution.

The first expansion group is:

- United States
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- New Zealand
- Singapore
- Hong Kong

Taiwan remains available.

## 2. Considered Approaches

### A. Open every supported country at once

This maximizes theoretical reach but also exposes an untranslated listing, unreviewed regional prices, and country-specific compliance obligations in many markets at once. It makes early performance and support signals harder to interpret.

### B. Expand to seven English-language markets first

This is the selected approach. The app already has English UI resources and English legal pages. A complete English Play listing and a controlled set of regional subscription prices can support these markets without changing the verified Billing or RTDN implementation.

### C. Keep Taiwan-only availability until a future Android release

This minimizes operational work but unnecessarily postpones market validation. Country and listing changes do not require a new AAB and therefore do not require an API 36 build.

## 3. Scope

The expansion will:

- add a complete English Play store listing;
- reuse the approved app icon, feature graphic, and four screenshots when they contain no misleading locale-specific text;
- review the monthly and annual price shown for every selected market;
- extend monthly, annual, and eligible monthly `trial10d` regional availability consistently;
- add the seven countries/regions to Production only after the listing and prices are ready;
- preserve Taiwan availability and its existing TWD prices;
- verify the public listing after Google Play publishes the changes.

The expansion will not:

- upload a new AAB or change version `1.0.8` / version code `6`;
- change Billing, backend verification, KMS, Firestore, acknowledgement, entitlement, or RTDN behavior;
- change product ID `just_notes_premium`, base-plan IDs `monthly` and `annual`, the 10-day trial duration, or annual no-trial behavior;
- add advertising, analytics, or a new SDK;
- open Japan, South Korea, the EEA, or other markets during this phase;
- submit price or country changes without an action-time confirmation.

## 4. English Store Listing

### App name

`Just Notes`

### Short description

`Private, focused notes with reminders, drawing, OCR and Drive backup.`

### Full description

`Capture ideas without clutter. Just Notes keeps your notes on your device by default and gives you practical tools for writing, organizing, and finding them again.`

`Features:`

- `Create text notes, checklists, and drawings`
- `Organize notes with folders, pinning, search, and filters`
- `Set reminders for important notes`
- `Turn text in images into notes with OCR`
- `Import and export your notes`
- `Optionally sync and restore a private backup through your Google Drive account`
- `Protect access to the app with its privacy lock`

`Premium features are available through optional Google Play subscriptions. Google Play shows the controlling local price, trial eligibility, renewal date, taxes, and billing terms before purchase. The monthly plan may include a 10-day trial only for eligible accounts. The annual plan has no trial.`

`Your notes are not used for advertising or profiling. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.`

The final Play Console entry must remain within Google Play character limits and must not claim features that are absent from the current production build.

## 5. Regional Subscription Review

Before any publication action, Play Console must be inspected for the exact local prices and availability of:

- `monthly` with no offer;
- `monthly` / `trial10d`;
- `annual` with no offer.

Google-generated converted prices may be used only after they are displayed to Brian for review. No price is inferred from a currency conversion outside Play Console.

Required invariants:

- Taiwan remains TWD 33 monthly and TWD 330 annual.
- `trial10d` remains monthly-only and eligibility remains controlled by Google Play.
- Annual remains without a trial.
- Each selected market has both active base plans.
- The monthly trial offer covers the same selected markets as the monthly base plan.
- The purchase sheet remains the authoritative source for local price, tax, renewal, and eligibility.

## 6. Legal and Support Consistency

The English Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Account Deletion pages remain the canonical links. Before opening non-Taiwan markets, any Taiwan-only fixed-price wording in the Terms must be generalized so that it truthfully describes localized Google Play pricing while preserving the Taiwan prices in Play Console.

Support remains `yeh.shibang@gmail.com`.

## 7. Execution Order

1. Inspect the existing English translation state in Play Console.
2. Enter and save the English name, descriptions, and suitable graphic assets.
3. Inspect monthly, annual, and `trial10d` availability and generated local prices for all seven markets.
4. Record the exact price table and present it for approval.
5. If needed, update and publish the Terms wording so it is accurate for localized prices.
6. Prepare the seven-country Production availability change.
7. Obtain explicit final confirmation for the regional prices and public availability.
8. Submit the price, offer-region, listing, and country changes.
9. Verify that the Production listing is available in each selected country and that Taiwan remains available.

## 8. Observation Plan

For two to four weeks after publication, review:

- first-time installers;
- acquired users;
- store-listing visitors and conversion rate;
- active devices/users;
- crashes and ANRs;
- monthly and annual subscription starts;
- trial starts and conversions, where Google Play provides sufficient data;
- cancellations and refund signals;
- backend verification, acknowledgement, RTDN retry, and DLQ health.

Low-volume or privacy-threshold-suppressed data must be reported as unavailable, not interpreted as zero.

## 9. Success Criteria

The phase is complete when:

- the English listing is published and accurate;
- Taiwan plus the seven selected markets are available in Production;
- every selected market has reviewed monthly and annual prices;
- monthly `trial10d` and annual no-trial behavior remain unchanged;
- no new AAB or backend revision was deployed;
- the public listing and subscription purchase sheet are spot-checked successfully;
- a dated two-to-four-week observation checkpoint is recorded.
