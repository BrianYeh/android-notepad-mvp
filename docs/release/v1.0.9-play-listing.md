### Full description

```text
想到就記，需要時找得到。

Just Notes 是一款快速、簡潔、重視隱私的筆記工具。筆記預設儲存在你的裝置上，不用建立帳戶就能開始。只有在你主動開啟時，才會使用 Google Drive 私人應用程式資料空間進行同步與還原。

每天更快開始
• 用「今天」查看逾期提醒、今日到期、釘選筆記和最近內容
• 使用每日待辦、購物、會議或課堂、日記、旅行準備和重要日期等內建範本
• 從 App 或桌面小工具建立筆記
• 編輯時自動儲存

需要時找得到
• 搜尋筆記標題與內容
• 搜尋能更自然地處理空格、大小寫和重音符號
• 使用釘選、篩選和提醒日曆整理資訊
• 透過裝置端 OCR 將圖片文字轉成筆記

替重要事項設定提醒
• 支援單次、每天、每週或每月提醒
• 在提醒日曆集中查看
• 通知可延後 10 分鐘
• 可隱藏通知中的筆記內容

筆記預設留在你的裝置
• Google Drive 同步完全由你選擇
• 同步資料使用你帳戶中的私人 appDataFolder
• 支援本機匯入、匯出與備份
• 可使用 Android 裝置螢幕鎖保護 App

免費功能包含文字筆記、結構化待辦清單、手繪、搜尋、提醒、日曆、範本、本機儲存與 Google Drive 同步。

Premium 提供資料夾與進階文字格式。訂閱由 Google Play 處理；當地價格、稅金、試用資格、續訂日期與付款條款以 Google Play 購買畫面為準。月方案僅可能向符合資格的帳戶提供 10 天試用，年方案沒有試用。

Just Notes 不含廣告，也不使用筆記內容進行廣告、分析、使用者剖析或販售。詳細資料處理方式請參閱隱私權政策。
```

### Full description

```text
Capture it now. Find it when it matters.

Just Notes is a fast, focused note app built around quick capture, on-device privacy by default, useful search, and practical reminders. You can start without creating an account. Optional Google Drive sync is available only when you choose it.

Start faster every day
• Use Today to see overdue reminders, items due today, pinned notes, and recent work
• Start from built-in templates for daily tasks, shopping, meetings or classes, journaling, trip preparation, and important dates
• Create notes from the app or home-screen widget
• Autosave while you work

Find it again
• Search note titles and content
• Search handles spacing, letter case, and accents more naturally
• Use pinning, filters, and the reminder calendar
• Turn text in images into notes with on-device OCR

Set useful reminders
• Choose once, daily, weekly, or monthly reminders
• Browse reminders in a calendar
• Snooze for 10 minutes
• Hide note content from reminder notifications

Private by default
• Notes are stored locally on your device by default
• Google Drive sync is optional
• Sync uses the private appDataFolder in your Google Drive account
• Import, export, and create local backups
• Protect the app with your Android device screen lock

Free features include text notes, structured checklists, drawings, search, reminders, calendar, templates, local storage, and Google Drive sync.

Premium adds folders and rich text formatting. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The purchase sheet shows the controlling local price, taxes, trial eligibility, renewal date, and billing terms. The monthly plan may include a 10-day trial only for eligible accounts. The annual plan has no trial.

Just Notes contains no advertising and does not use note content for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sale. See the Privacy Policy for details.
```

### Release notes

```text
Meet Today and six quick-start templates. First use is clearer, and search now handles imperfect spacing and accents more naturally. Notes still stay on-device by default, with no ads or content analytics.
```
